# Changelog

What's new in Ziman·Zabân, newest first. To add an entry, follow [the changelog skill](.cursor/skills/changelog/SKILL.md).

## 2026-11-08

- App: native speakers can record words and sentences on the new "Record voices" page in the menu. They read one text after another, and the app saves a recording at each pause and shows the next text.
- Audio: a word or sentence recorded by a native speaker plays before the computer voice.
- Audio: all 390 Sorani words are now recorded by Zino Kader, a native speaker. Sentences, questions and answers still use the computer voice.
- Method & sources: "Sources & licenses" lists each native speaker who recorded clips, and how many.

## 2026-11-07

- Recall: you always answer by picking one of four options. No typing and no extra click.
- Recall: to type or say the answer instead, pick the "Type" or "Say" tab. "Options" takes you back. The choice is also in Settings.
- App: when a session ends, the app suggests a few new words, even if you still have words to review. The button starts a session with only new words.
- App: when there's nothing to review, the home page asks if you want to learn some new words.
- Progress: you get a medal at 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200 and 250 words learned, and when you've learned every word in the course.
- App: when you reach a medal, the app celebrates with confetti and shows your new medal.
- Progress: "Medals" shows the medals you have and how many words are left to the next ones. Medals for words you learned before today arrive after your next session.

## 2026-11-02

- Recognition: say the meaning out loud instead of typing it.
- Recall: new words start as a choice between four options. Once you've picked a word twice, you type or say it.
- Recall: stuck on a word? Press "Show 4 options". The word then comes back a little sooner.
- Recall: choose "Type" or "Say" for your answers. "Say" records you so you can compare with the model answer. The choice is also in Settings.
- Recall: in Persian, you can dictate your answer instead of typing it.
- Q&A: answers, options and word tiles only use words you have already met.
- Module pages: a new card explains when words come back for review, and shows how many come back later today, tomorrow, this week and later.
- Module pages: the card also says what happens once you've learned every word.
- Audio: Persian sentences, questions and answers have a new voice.
- App: "What's new" in the menu lists changes like these, and the home page tells you when there are new ones.
