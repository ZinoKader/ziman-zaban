# Changelog

What's new in Ziman·Zabân, newest first. To add an entry, follow [the changelog skill](.cursor/skills/changelog/SKILL.md).

## 2026-11-16

- Audio: almost every Persian sentence, question and answer is now recorded by Essi, 886 new clips in all. Sorani sentences still use the computer voice.
- App: the text in menus, exercises, grammar notes and word notes is rewritten to read more naturally, most of all in Swedish.
- App: in Swedish, Recall is now called "Minnas" and the button that checks your answer says "Rätta".
- Q&A: example answers no longer make broken sentences like "I want one eggs" or "I work at Stockholm", and they always start with a capital letter.
- Q&A: Persian has its own question "When do you study Persian?". Before, the Persian version was shown as "When do you study Kurdish?".
- Grammar: some explanations are corrected. For example, Tehran Persian says shir for both "lion" and "milk".
- Progress: the "Activity" chart shows a week, a month or a year. The arrows go back in time, and you pick Answers, Minutes, Accuracy or New to chart. Each number is compared with the period before.
- Progress: "Your year" shows every day of the last 12 months as a square, darker the more you practised. Tap a day, or a bar in "Activity", to open it.
- Progress: "Day by day" lists the sessions of the chosen day and every answer in them: the time, the word or sentence, right or wrong, and how long it took. "Mistakes" shows only the wrong answers.
- Progress: new cards show which modules you used, which weekdays and hours you practise, the words you have met and learned over time, and your personal records.
- Progress: a new "Memory" card shows how many of your learned words are sticking right now, which are fading, and how long they last before they need a review. It also shows how often you were right on reviews in the last 30 days.
- Progress: the page has a new order. Your course, your memory and the reviews coming up are at the top, and the history is below. In "Day by day", a session stays closed until you open it.
- App: the top bar no longer runs past the edge of laptop screens. Picture recognition is in it only on very wide screens.

## 2026-11-14

- Picture recognition: a new module next to the path. It shows a photo, asks "What do you see?" and gives four Sorani or Persian words to pick from. There are 63 photos in five areas: dishes like ghormeh sabzi and kebab koobideh, the haft-sin table, the pantry, the market and a café.
- Picture recognition: a new picture is shown with its word and audio first. If you pick the wrong word, the answer shows what that word means.
- App: Picture recognition is on the home page under "More practice", in the menu, and in the top bar on wide screens.

## 2026-11-12

- App: progress is saved to an account online after every answer. Before, it was only saved in the browser. The first time the app opens, the progress already on the device is added to the account.
- Settings: "Create account" links your progress to your email and a password. "Sign in" opens it on another device, and what you practised there as a guest is added to it.
- Settings: your settings follow the account to other devices.
- Settings: "Import backup" adds the backup to your progress instead of replacing it.
- App: the account button at the top of the page opens a small form to create an account or sign in. Once you're signed in, it shows the first letter of your email and has "Sign out".
- App: after a practice session, guests are asked to create an account. "Not now" hides the question for a week.

## 2026-11-08

- App: native speakers can record words and sentences on the new "Record voices" page in the menu. They read one text after another, and the app saves a recording at each pause and shows the next text.
- Audio: a word or sentence recorded by a native speaker plays before the computer voice.
- Audio: all 390 Sorani words are now recorded by Zino Kader, a native speaker. Sentences, questions and answers still use the computer voice.
- Audio: all 424 Persian words are now recorded by Essi, a native speaker. Sentences, questions and answers still use the computer voice.
- Method & sources: "Sources & licenses" lists each native speaker who recorded clips, and how many.
- Record voices: "Start recording" first runs a microphone check. You say one word, hear it played back, and see what to fix if the microphone doesn't work. "Test microphone" runs it again.
- Record voices: on iPhone the microphone now starts from your tap. Before, it was allowed but the app heard nothing.
- Record voices: recording on iPhone keeps working in silent mode.
- Record voices: you no longer need to hold the phone close. The app only says a recording is quiet when your voice is close to the background noise, and recordings play back at full volume.
- Record voices: "Start recording" is a full-width button again, with "Test microphone" below it.

## 2026-11-07

- Recall: you always answer by picking one of four options. No typing and no extra click.
- Recall: to type or say the answer instead, pick the "Type" or "Say" tab. "Options" takes you back. The choice is also in Settings.
- App: when a session ends, the app suggests a few new words, even if you still have words to review. The button starts a session with only new words.
- App: when there's nothing to review, the home page asks if you want to learn some new words.
- Progress: you get a medal at 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200 and 250 words learned, and when you've learned every word in the course.
- App: when you reach a medal, the app celebrates with confetti and shows your new medal.
- Progress: "Medals" shows the medals you have and how many words are left to the next ones. Medals for words you learned before today arrive after your next session.

## 2026-11-02

- Recognition: say the meaning out loud instead of typing it.
- Recall: new words start as a choice between four options. Once you've picked a word twice, you type or say it.
- Recall: stuck on a word? Press "Show 4 options". The word then comes back a little sooner.
- Recall: choose "Type" or "Say" for your answers. "Say" records you so you can compare with the model answer. The choice is also in Settings.
- Recall: in Persian, you can dictate your answer instead of typing it.
- Q&A: answers, options and word tiles only use words you have already met.
- Module pages: a new card explains when words come back for review, and shows how many come back later today, tomorrow, this week and later.
- Module pages: the card also says what happens once you've learned every word.
- Audio: Persian sentences, questions and answers have a new voice.
- App: "What's new" in the menu lists changes like these, and the home page tells you when there are new ones.
